===Mercenaries===
The [[2023 Mercenaries Crowdfunding Campaign]] released one box set, sixteen [[ForcePacks]], and five [[BattleTech Salvage Box|Salvage Boxes]] consisting of Fifty four [[BattleMech]]s, twenty seven [[combat vehicle]]s, one [[aerospace fighter]], and one [[battlearmor]] type.{{cn}}

Table Notes: The column denoted "Parts" gives the number of parts per box set for each miniature prior to any required assembly. The column denoted "Catalog Number" can show different product codes if the miniature was available in different boxes. The manufacturer for the Mercenaries Kickstarter is [[Liya International]], Catalyst Game Labs remains the license holder through Topps.{{cn}}
