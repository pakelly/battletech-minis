===BattleTech Clan Invasion===

Further miniatures are being produced in [[BattleTech: Clan Invasion]], published in 2020, along with related [[ForcePack]]s and [[Salvage Box]]es.
Similar to miniatures from the [[BattleTech Beginner Box]] and [[BattleTech: A Game of Armored Combat]], these were molded in gray plastic and then attached to a black plastic hex base.  Models in this line have a small number printed on the underside of the base.

Table Notes: The column denoted "Parts" gives the number of parts per box set for each miniature prior to assembly. The column denoted "Catalog Number" can show different product codes if the miniature was available in different boxes.
